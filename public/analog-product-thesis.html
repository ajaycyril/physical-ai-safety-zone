01 / THE PRODUCT THESIS

The moat is not a model.
It is the closed loop.

Frontier models will commoditize. Robot hardware will diversify. Durable advantage comes from owning how reality is represented, how actions are governed and how outcomes return as evidence.

“Observe the physical world. Establish trusted state. Reason and simulate. Authorize action. Execute. Verify. Learn.”
01
SENSE

Observe

Robots, cameras, IoT, BIM, GIS, SCADA, BMS and enterprise systems continuously report what is happening.

02
GROUND

Trust

Identity, time, location, source, provenance and confidence turn raw signals into authoritative operational state.

03
REASON

Understand

Rules, forecasts, probabilistic planning and learned models explain conditions and propose plausible futures.

04
GOVERN

Authorize

Policy, permissions, risk, human approval and safety constraints determine what may happen next.

05
ORCHESTRATE

Act

People, robots and software systems execute constrained missions through certified control layers.

06
PROVE

Verify

Evidence links action to asset, work order and outcome — then improves the next decision.

06 / PRODUCT ROADMAP

Make truth reusable.
Make operations repeatable.
Make intelligence compound.

NOW0–6MONTHS

PHASE 01

Trust + one repeatable workflow

BUILD

Canonical ontology, state classes, provenance, mission / policy / evidence contracts, one inspection template and real unit economics.

EXIT SIGNAL

The same mission and evidence model works across multiple sites, with traceable state and a measured customer outcome.

NEXT6–18MONTHS

PHASE 02

Repeatability across estates

BUILD

Estate templates, adapters, developer portal, partner certification, model gateway, simulation regression and governed cross-estate learning.

EXIT SIGNAL

Most implementation effort uses reusable product components; expansion comes from adding sites and workflows, not rebuilding.

THEN18–36MONTHS

PHASE 03

Compounding autonomy

BUILD

Domain action models, multi-agent planning, bounded closed loops, a partner marketplace, outcome-based contracts and federated intelligence.

EXIT SIGNAL

Every completed mission improves deployment speed, model quality, service margin and the defensibility of the platform.

RECOMMENDED FIRST WEDGE

Autonomous inspection and assurance for critical and public-realm assets.

Repeated, bounded, measurable missions create the cleanest path from demonstration to product — and from data collection to a proprietary learning loop.
